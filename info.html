Info

You get a photo from BRM5 and have to guess where it was taken on the map. Play 5 rounds and try to get the highest score.

  • Guessing: Click the map to place your pin, click again to move it, then press Guess.
  • Scoring: Each round is worth up to 500 points. Land within 20 meters for the full score, and the points drop the farther away you are.
  • Difficulty: Every photo is labeled Easy, Medium, Hard or Extreme in the sidebar. It is only a label and does not change your score.
  • Zooming the map: Scroll to zoom in and drag to move around. You can't zoom out or drag past the edge of the map.
  • Rotating the map: Hold right click on the map and drag left or right, or use the Rotation slider. The rotation resets every round.
  • Looking closer at the photo: While the photo is in the corner, hover over it to zoom in on the spot under your cursor.
  • Swapping the photo and map: Click the photo to swap places with the map, so the photo is big and the map is small. Click the photo or the small map to swap back. When the photo is big, scroll to zoom and drag to move around it. Pressing Guess or starting the next round swaps back automatically.
  • Results: After you guess, the map shows your pin, the real spot in green and a line between them. The sidebar shows the distance, your points and both grid coordinates.
  • Gallery: Open the Gallery from the homepage to browse every photo, sorted by difficulty, and filter by map.
